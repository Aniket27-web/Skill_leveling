## Recording notes

- **Account Setup**: Use an active user account with credentials, a pre-saved resume, and at least 2 completed sessions already logged, ensuring the Score Trend chart and Raid History show rich data (e.g., Level 24 Awakened Hunter, D-Rank, scores in the 75–85 range).
- **Emphasize Ranks & Scores Aloud**: Keep verbal delivery confident when naming ranks (E-Rank Weakest, D-Rank, C-Rank, B-Rank, A-Rank, S-Rank Sovereign) and specific scores (e.g. "scored 84 out of 100 across Relevance, Accuracy, Depth, and Clarity").
- **Highlight Visual Cues**: When the voice-over mentions the Hunter Status Bar, cursor-hover over the Level and XP bar. On the debrief screen, linger for 2 seconds on the overall score ring and dimensional breakdown.
- **Pacing**: Use quick cuts during network waits (resume parsing, question generation, and report evaluation) to keep the video concise and tightly locked within the 2:50–2:58 target runtime.
